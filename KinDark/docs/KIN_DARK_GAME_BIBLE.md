# Kin Dark Master Book

## Project Frame

- Game: Kin Dark
- Format: three-protagonist action RPG staged in true simulated 3D space with 2D high-definition comic-book sprite prefabs for actors and sprite-assembled 3D environment forms.
- Tone: light-hearted gallows humor, grim urban grotesque, morally charged survival comedy.
- Structural promise: one giant contiguous city-map hosting three intertwined timelines, with route convergence rather than isolated campaigns.
- Total planned campaign duration: 300 hours across 12 arcs.

## ScanTide-Derived Story Engine

Kin Dark uses a ScanTide-derived wave process rather than flat chapter outlining. Each major arc is scored across seven pressure channels:

- witness clarity
- dread pressure
- myth breach
- bodily peril
- institutional pressure
- absurdity spark
- closure heat

Those pressures are used to decide which protagonist drives the arc, how the city shifts, and where the player timelines intersect.

## Protagonists

### Moe

- Role: recently divorced vacuum cleaner salesman turned pistol-and-flashlight survivor
- Look: solemn, balding, stubbled, wearing a stained ribbed undershirt, scraggly cut-off shorts, and brown loafers
- Timeline premise: Moe starts in the low-rent apartment belt and follows the mythicozoocryptid outbreak through debt collectors, failed marriages, ruined retail blocks, and institutional cover-ups.
- Combat lane: light sidearm gunplay, flashlight cone management, desperate close-range scrapping, and recoil-heavy finishers
- Traversal lane: door breaching, ledge vaulting, flashlight tracing, shove interactions, and grounded urban scrambling
- Signature tools: vacuum-salesman sample case, flashlight, pistol, receipt ledger
- 12-directional animation contract: idle, walk, run, aim_reticle, flashlight_scan, quickdraw, pistol_fire, reload, dodge_roll, vault, inspect, hold_interact

### Yil

- Role: gnome mechanic-magician who can rewrite broken infrastructure with ritual engineering
- Look: small and sharp-eyed, dressed in a lavish robe, tri-cornered hat, broad mustache, curled leather boots, and a black belt with a spiral buckle
- Timeline premise: Yil enters from the sewer workshops and abandoned machine sanctums, tracking why the city’s utility grid is acting like a summoning lattice.
- Combat lane: rune-charged casts, turret sigils, reality hinge repairs, area denial glyphs, and burst-navigation spells
- Traversal lane: blink stepping, levitation rails, rune climbing, machinery bridging, and magical short-range glide control
- Signature tools: spiral wrench, glyph satchel, lantern capacitor, tricorn relay pins
- 12-directional animation contract: idle, walk, run, aim_spell, wrench_cast, sigil_burst, ward_raise, blink_step, glide_rune, mechanic_climb, inspect, hold_interact

### Lou

- Role: private investigator monkey who avoids direct combat and puppets threats through telepathic control
- Look: wiry and observant, wearing a trench coat, sneakers, and a detective’s shoulder holster full of notebooks and signal tags
- Timeline premise: Lou begins on the rooftops and transit signage, tracing who benefits from the panic while literally reading intent off the city’s frightened population.
- Combat lane: telepathic domination, enemy hijack chains, crowd steering, stealth disruption, and evidence-tagging misdirection
- Traversal lane: all-surface climbing, vent slipping, pipe swinging, perch hopping, and remote interaction through mind-thread focus
- Signature tools: telepathic lens, detective notebook, signal tags, grapnel cord
- 12-directional animation contract: idle, walk, run, aim_focus, telepathic_mark, mind_puppet, command_release, wall_climb, ceiling_traverse, perch_leap, inspect, hold_interact

## World Geography

The city is one giant nightmare metropolis, built from ruined housing, collapsed civic space, sewer infrastructure, dead factories, broken marinas, reliquary museums, and transit rings. It is expressionistically over-detailed, dense with light, moisture, soot, signage, fungus, relic junk, and artificially photographed texture surfaces mapped onto 3D model forms.

- Vacancy Row: boarded rental towers, divorce courts, pawn stores, radiator alleys
- Floodcourt Mile: rain-choked boulevards, submerged crosswalks, courthouse drains
- Tin Saint Market: collapsed shopping arcades, shrine kiosks, neon awnings
- Rookery Switch: elevated train wrecks, bird-haunted signal gantries, soot balconies
- Candlelung Towers: art deco residential stacks, dangling bridges, blackouts
- Siltglass Sewers: runoff tunnels, ritual pumps, fungal sluices
- Mothline Works: abandoned textile automation lines, belt systems, lint storms
- Choirburn Foundry: steel halls, slag altars, derailed loaders, boiler cathedrals
- Pilgrim Underpass: pilgrim camps, reliquary traffic, graffiti chapels
- Spite Marina: dry docks, buckled marinas, salt-drenched motels
- Split Reliquary: museum vaults, broken exhibits, saint-tech archives
- Ninth Station: final transit ring, cracked observatory roof, reality-fissure core

## Plot Arcs

### Arc 01: Vacancy Notices

- Estimated hours: 18
- Focus route: Moe
- Lead district: Vacancy Row
- Premise: The city starts with evictions, unpaid debts, and the first cryptids slipping through leaking apartment walls.
- Moral collision: Moe mistakes the outbreak for a neighborhood scam until a mythic intruder takes one of his sales routes apart from the inside.
- Arc outcome: Moe survives long enough to decide the city is lying about what is happening.

### Arc 02: Wet Law

- Estimated hours: 20
- Focus route: Moe
- Lead district: Floodcourt Mile
- Premise: Courthouse runoff, divorce archives, and drowned clerks turn legal paperwork into a monster habitat.
- Moral collision: Moe chases proof of his ex-wife's disappearance and discovers the court feeds disappearances into a municipal secrecy machine.
- Arc outcome: The player learns the outbreak follows institutional shame as much as literal cracks.

### Arc 03: Wrench Gospel

- Estimated hours: 22
- Focus route: Yil
- Lead district: Siltglass Sewers
- Premise: Yil uncovers that maintenance tunnels are acting like spell diagrams for the influx.
- Moral collision: Repairing the city speeds the invasion unless the repairs are morally chosen instead of mechanically efficient.
- Arc outcome: Yil becomes responsible for deciding which parts of the city deserve power.

### Arc 04: Rookery Contradictions

- Estimated hours: 24
- Focus route: Lou
- Lead district: Rookery Switch
- Premise: Lou climbs the signal gantries and discovers that public fear is being tuned like radio traffic.
- Moral collision: Enemy behavior can be redirected, but every use of telepathic control leaves ethical residue in witnesses and controlled victims.
- Arc outcome: Lou finds the first proof that someone human wants the mythicozoocryptids to stay.

### Arc 05: Market of False Saints

- Estimated hours: 26
- Focus route: Yil
- Lead district: Tin Saint Market
- Premise: The central shopping district becomes a carnival of counterfeit relics and living saint mannequins.
- Moral collision: Yil's salvage politics collide with black-market miracle dealers who can temporarily domesticate monsters.
- Arc outcome: The game opens up multi-timeline trading, sabotage, and faction reputation loops.

### Arc 06: Factory Choir

- Estimated hours: 28
- Focus route: Moe
- Lead district: Choirburn Foundry
- Premise: Moe chases ammunition, only to find the factories are forging liturgical machine-beasts.
- Moral collision: The foundry leadership treats workers and monsters as interchangeable production inputs.
- Arc outcome: Moe's pistol path evolves into a grim industrial survival campaign with heavy moral choices.

### Arc 07: Underpass Parliament

- Estimated hours: 24
- Focus route: Lou
- Lead district: Pilgrim Underpass
- Premise: Lou discovers encampments where humans and myth-creatures negotiate survival without the city's permission.
- Moral collision: Every mind-control shortcut risks destroying the only fragile diplomacy keeping the underpass from becoming a massacre.
- Arc outcome: Lou's route becomes the social and investigative spine of the whole game.

### Arc 08: Marina of Bad Echoes

- Estimated hours: 26
- Focus route: Moe
- Lead district: Spite Marina
- Premise: The docks reveal a cargo route that imported reality fractures as insurance fraud.
- Moral collision: Moe has to choose between revenge, rescue, and burning the shipping conspiracy alive.
- Arc outcome: Sea-adjacent horrors and flood-bosses widen the city into a nightmare coast.

### Arc 09: Reliquary Weather

- Estimated hours: 28
- Focus route: Yil
- Lead district: Split Reliquary
- Premise: Museum archives and saint-tech storage vaults show that the city has rehearsed this crisis before.
- Moral collision: Yil's magic navigation and repair abilities uncover a hidden urban cosmology built out of maintenance protocols.
- Arc outcome: The story pivots from outbreak response to reality-governance collapse.

### Arc 10: Mutual Vacancy

- Estimated hours: 30
- Focus route: Lou
- Lead district: Candlelung Towers
- Premise: The apartment high-rises become a war of tenants, landlords, rescue cults, and controlled cryptids.
- Moral collision: Lou can keep the towers from total annihilation, but only by making ethically compromising alliances.
- Arc outcome: This arc ties all three protagonists' private grief into the larger citywide catastrophe.

### Arc 11: Ninth Signal

- Estimated hours: 26
- Focus route: All
- Lead district: Ninth Station
- Premise: Every timeline converges on the transit ring where the city keeps its final shuttered observatory and largest fissure engine.
- Moral collision: Boss institutions openly weaponize the outbreak while the protagonists start crossing each other's routes in real time.
- Arc outcome: The game's systems begin recombining all three traversal/combat languages.

### Arc 12: Kin Dark

- Estimated hours: 28
- Focus route: All
- Lead district: Ninth Station
- Premise: The final act forces the protagonists to decide what a city owes its monsters, victims, and guilty institutions once the truth can no longer be hidden.
- Moral collision: The last bosses are as much institutions and memories as biological enemies, and the moral endgame is survival without innocence.
- Arc outcome: The ending resolves the three timelines into one transformed city-state or one spectacular urban ruin.

## Enemy Speciation And Biology

The mythicozoocryptids are not random monsters. They are ecological opportunists drawn to stress, secrecy, debt, ritual waste, and architectural neglect. Their biology fuses urban residue with cryptid myth, making them both comic and grotesque.

- Stamplick Hounds: receipt-paper canids that track debt, not scent.
- Umbra Cranes: skeletal industrial birds that nest in lifted scaffolds.
- Radiator Cherubs: steam-bloated hallway imps that scream through vents.
- Ticket Leeches: rail-thin parasites that staple themselves to fare gates.
- Sump Gloamers: blind eel-mollusk hybrids that pulse at pipe pressure changes.
- Halo Vermin: halo-ringed rats bred by reality fissures and billboard light.
- Glassback Croakers: frogish sewer prophets with mirrored spines.
- Latch Monkeys: lock-picking gutter apes that mob in laughing packs.
- Invoice Beetles: lacquered roaches that digest archive dust and glue.
- Bell Wives: bridal mantis-things that assemble from drapery and rebar.
- Floodcourt Colts: wet horse-jackals that gallop through courthouse water tables.
- Tin Saints: counterfeit saint-statues animated by civic panic.

## Systems And Timeline Rules

- The player can begin as any protagonist, but the city timeline continues for all three.
- Moe resolves immediate bodily danger and exposes civic abuse.
- Yil repairs, reroutes, and re-enchants the city, which changes traversal and faction access for everyone.
- Lou controls enemies, reads crowd intent, and opens social stealth routes other protagonists cannot access.
- Timeline collisions rewrite future mission states, boss availability, shop inventories, rumor networks, and safe routes.
- Every interaction has a dedicated VFX animation family and a matching systemic response class.

## Animation, Combat, And Traversal Contract

- Every protagonist ships with 12 unique animation sets, authored for 8-way omnidirectional facing.
- Every NPC, enemy, and boss also resolves through omnidirectional facing, camera-relative reticle logic, and readable telegraphs.
- Combat is real-time and controller-first.
- The camera stays squarely behind the active protagonist, follows right-stick reticle orientation, and zooms for lock-on while LT is held.
- Interaction is auto-contextualized onto the A button and requires a one-second hold when an interactable target is in focus.
