# Kaiju Gaiden Boss Minion Ecology Profiles

This file defines the minion families that should accompany each boss so encounters read as ecosystems instead of one shared generic roster.

Shared production rule:

- Every boss should ship with at least three minion reads: a pressure body, a harassment body, and a support or brood body.
- Each minion family should inherit one obvious anatomical trait from its boss and one smaller biome-specific mutation trait.

## Harbor Leviathan

- Family: reef spitters, drowned scavengers, hook-jaw tide feeders.
- Anatomy: goby-eel, crab, and drowned canine logic.
- Behavior read: peck pressure, lane clutter, wake-feeding.
- Combat split: ranged spitters, hook-jaw flankers, low tide carrion feeders.

## Cinder Wyrm

- Family: coal-hounds, ash skinks, pyre carrion striders.
- Anatomy: lizard and feral dog logic, scorched ribs, ember throats.
- Behavior read: heat feints, smoke harassment, pack lunges.
- Combat split: ash skink dart units, coal-hound bruisers, smoke-cough harassment striders.

## Root Bastion

- Family: root den lurkers, bark boars, fungal offshoot sentries.
- Anatomy: boar, monitor, and mangrove offshoot logic.
- Behavior read: flank from cover, root braces, pin lanes.
- Combat split: bark boar pushers, root lurker flankers, fungal sentry lane lockers.

## Glacier Maw

- Family: frost wolves, seal scavengers, whiteout gnashers.
- Anatomy: wolf, fox, seal, and starvation-cat logic.
- Behavior read: rush windows, edge checks, snow-blind pressure.
- Combat split: frost wolf rushers, seal scavenger harriers, whiteout gnashers for scramble pressure.

## Vault Serpent

- Family: crypt choir blinders, bone eels, reliquary leeches.
- Anatomy: lamprey, monk-fish, skeletal retainers.
- Behavior read: gateholding, line denial, ritual swarms.
- Combat split: blind choir blockers, bone eel lashes, reliquary leech supports.

## Shard Colossus

- Family: shard mimics, cutglass calves, reflective tendon skaters.
- Anatomy: hoofed younglings plus mineral parasite growth.
- Behavior read: diagonal cuts, false duplicates, sudden split lines.
- Combat split: shard mimic decoys, tendon skaters, cutglass calves that break lanes.

## Logora Raptormoth

- Family: nest raptors, storm gliders, crest moth scavengers.
- Anatomy: cassowary juveniles, gull hawks, moth-tailed gliders.
- Behavior read: high-low feints, flank drops, wingbeat crowding.
- Combat split: nest raptor rushers, crest moth clutter units, storm glider drop attackers.

## Basalt Tyrant

- Family: toll brutes, vent toads, slag-backed tunnel apes.
- Anatomy: toad, ape, and kiln-lizard logic.
- Behavior read: walling, body checks, corridor tax pressure.
- Combat split: toll brute walls, vent toad disruptors, slag-backed tunnel ape heavies.

## Goolloki Moonfrog

- Family: grub frogs, bloom tadpole hunters, gland-spore toadlets.
- Anatomy: larval frogs, salamanders, and swamp eel hatchlings.
- Behavior read: herd pressure, jump bursts, lung-clog clustering.
- Combat split: grub frog swarmers, bloom tadpole leapers, gland-spore cloggers.

## Dust Oracle

- Family: omen eyes, sand augurs, veil-wing scavengers.
- Anatomy: carrion birds, lizards, and detached sensory brood.
- Behavior read: range peeling, scouting loops, omen-mark harassment.
- Combat split: omen eye scouts, sand augur peelers, veil-wing harriers.

## Night Abyss

- Family: abyss drones, lurefish parasites, surf leeches.
- Anatomy: lampfish, gulper fry, oil-black eel spawn.
- Behavior read: undertow pressure, visibility denial, tide swarm behavior.
- Combat split: abyss drone swarmers, lurefish distractors, surf leech visibility-denial bodies.

## Crown Behemoth

- Family: bone court elites, embalmed hounds, ossuary pages.
- Anatomy: court-beast retainers, half-resurrected vassals, ceremonial carrion animals.
- Behavior read: formation discipline, escort pressure, throne-lock control.
- Combat split: bone court elites, embalmed hound escorts, ossuary page support units.
